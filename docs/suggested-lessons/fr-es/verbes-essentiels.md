# Verbes essentiels

Base language: Spanish. Target: French. 30 high-frequency verbs, présent
tense, mostly irregular. See the [shared mechanics](../README.md#shared-mechanics).

## Lessons

| # | Title | Prompt → answer | Instruction (es) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Significado (es → fr) | Spanish meaning → French infinitive | Escribe el infinitivo en francés. |
| 2 | Significado (fr → es) | French infinitive → Spanish meaning (any accepted alternative) | Escribe el significado en español. |
| final | Conjugación | Sentence with a gap (`Tu _____ le bus.` + infinitive tag) → conjugated form; every verb × `je tu il elle on nous vous ils elles` | Completa la frase con la forma correcta del verbo. |

Special cases:
- **falloir** is impersonal: only `il faut`, with the note "verbo impersonal:
  solo se conjuga con « il »". Its multiple-choice distractors are filled from
  all other présent forms.
- **essayer** accepts both spellings: essaie/essaye, essaies/essayes,
  essaie/essaye, essaient/essayent.
- `Je` becomes `J’` when the je form starts with a vowel (j'ai, j'aime, j'oublie,
  j'attends, j'essaie, j'apprends).
