# Verbes en -er

Base language: Spanish. Target: French. 50 first-group verbs, présent tense.
See the [shared mechanics](../README.md#shared-mechanics).

## Lessons

| # | Title | Prompt → answer | Instruction (es) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Significado (es → fr) | Spanish meaning → French infinitive | Escribe el infinitivo en francés. |
| 2 | Significado (fr → es) | French infinitive → Spanish meaning (any accepted alternative) | Escribe el significado en español. |
| final | Conjugación | Sentence with a gap: `Nous _____ les valises dans la voiture.` + infinitive tag → conjugated form; every verb × `je tu il elle on nous vous ils elles` | Completa la frase con la forma correcta del verbo. |

Multiple-choice distractors: other infinitives (1), other meanings (2), the
same verb's other présent forms (final).

## Conjugation rules

Stem = infinitive minus `-er`.

| type | je | tu | il/elle/on | nous | vous | ils/elles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| regular | stem+e | stem+es | stem+e | stem+ons | stem+ez | stem+ent |
| -ger | stem+e | stem+es | stem+e | stem+**eons** | stem+ez | stem+ent |
| -cer | stem+e | stem+es | stem+e | stem with c→**ç** +ons | stem+ez | stem+ent |

Exception: **protéger** is -ger *and* changes é→è before a silent ending:
protège, protèges, protège, protégeons, protégez, protègent.

Subject display: `Je` becomes `J’` before a vowel-initial verb (marked ✓ below).
